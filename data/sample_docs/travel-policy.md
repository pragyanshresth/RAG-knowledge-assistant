# Travel Policy
## 2.1 Reimbursement
Submit expense reports within 30 days of travel with itemized receipts. Economy airfare, standard hotel rooms and ground transport are reimbursed in full. Meals are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
## 2.2 Pre-approval
Trips over $1,500 require VP approval before booking.
