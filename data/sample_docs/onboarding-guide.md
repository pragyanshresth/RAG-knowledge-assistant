# Onboarding Guide
## 1.1 Employees
New employees complete HR paperwork on day one, attend orientation in week one, and meet their buddy and manager for a 30-60-90 day plan.
## 1.2 Contractors
Contractors are onboarded by their sponsoring manager: sign the NDA and statement of work, receive a time-limited account (maximum 6 months, renewable), and finish security awareness training before accessing any system.
